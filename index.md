# email-organiser

A personal tool that sorts my own email into labels and folders using a locally hosted AI model.

## Privacy policy

This app is used only by its developer, on the developer's own Google accounts. It reads email metadata and content from those accounts so a locally hosted model can categorise them. All processing happens on the developer's own hardware. No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and no data is used for advertising or for training models. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
